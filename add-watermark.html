FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about FixMyPDF privacy, offline processing, and free PDF tools.

Can I customize the opacity and rotation of the watermark?

Yes, adjust transparency, font size, color, angle, and repeat patterns in real time.

Can the watermark be easily removed by viewers?

The watermark is embedded directly into the PDF content stream for robust document protection.

Is FixMyPDF really safe and private?

Yes, 100%. All processing runs entirely client-side inside your browser using WebAssembly and pure JavaScript. Your documents never leave your device and are never transmitted to any external server or cloud storage.

Are there any file size limits, subscriptions, or hidden fees?

FixMyPDF is completely free forever. There are no subscriptions, no credit card requirements, no daily usage limits, and no watermarks added to your documents. File size capacity is limited only by your computer or phone memory.
